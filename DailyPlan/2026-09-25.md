### 【晨起热身·灵魂一问】

> **思考题**: 平面图形由 $y = x^2$ 与 $y = \sqrt{x}$ 围成。求该图形绕 $y$ 轴旋转一周所得旋转体的体积！
>
> <details>
> <summary><b>点击查看破题思考与提示</b></summary>
> 
> 1. **交点**: $(0,0)$ 与 $(1,1)$；
> 2. **圆柱壳法 (对 $x$ 积分)**:
>    $$V = 2\pi \int_0^1 x (\sqrt{x} - x^2) \mathrm{d}x = 2\pi \int_0^1 (x^{3/2} - x^3) \mathrm{d}x = 2\pi \left[\frac{2}{5} - \frac{1}{4}\right] = \frac{3\pi}{10}$$
> 3. **圆盘法 (对 $y$ 积分)**:
>    $$V = \pi \int_0^1 (x_{\text{外}}^2 - x_{\text{内}}^2) \mathrm{d}y = \pi \int_0^1 (y - y^4) \mathrm{d}y = \pi \left[\frac{1}{2} - \frac{1}{5}\right] = \frac{3\pi}{10}$$
> </details>
